Welcome! I'm Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUNLP) supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

I'm passionate about understanding how large language models think, reason, and how we can improve their reliability and interpretability.
